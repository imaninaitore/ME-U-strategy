# MEUstrategy

This website exists to motivate up coming entrepreneurs to believe in and achieve their business dream with the help of business strategy consultants.

**MEUstrategy** provides a platform to interact with business management and strategy consultants who can help just about anyone make their small business idea huge.
The goal is to educate and equip the entrepreneurs with knowledge and skills on business management and ensure quality service in the community.
